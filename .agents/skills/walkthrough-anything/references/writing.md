# Writing the copy

The viewer reads a card in the time it takes the cursor to settle. Copy that needs a second read has failed. These rules are not style preferences; they are what the hold-time budget allows.

## Card title (at most 6 words)

- States the one idea. "The verdict comes first." "Pick the expiring policy."
- Verb-led when the viewer acts; noun-led when the viewer looks.
- No punctuation except a full stop if it is a sentence.
- Never a question. Never a pun.

## Card body (1–2 sentences, at most 28 words)

- Sentence one: what the viewer is looking at or doing, in the app's own words.
- Sentence two: why it matters to them, or what happens next.
- Present tense. Second person when the viewer acts ("Tick the quotes"). Third person when the app acts ("Results save to Box").
- Concrete nouns from the interface. If the button says "Evaluate", the card says Evaluate.

## Chapter captions (at most 2 words)

Nouns that name the step of the job: "Documents", "Evaluate", "Compare". Numbered "2 / 4" so the viewer knows how far along they are.

## Title cards

- Opening: the logo or product name, and one line that sets expectation: "A 90-second walkthrough". Nothing else.
- Closing: the logo and the steps of the job in order. No sign-off line. The flow is the summary; "That's it" adds nothing.

## Banned

- Hedges and fillers: simply, just, easily, basically, actually, of course.
- Marketing adjectives: powerful, seamless, intuitive, beautiful, robust, smart.
- Promises about speed or effort you did not measure: "about a minute", "in seconds", "instantly".
- Meta-narration: "In this section we will", "As you can see", "Let's take a look".
- Exclamation marks. Emoji. Questions to the viewer.
- Describing the UI's appearance ("the blue button on the right"). The spotlight already points.

## Say the consequence, not the mechanism

Weak: "This button calls the evaluation endpoint for each selected quote."
Strong: "Each quote is compared with the expiring policy. Results save as they finish."

Weak: "Three tabs. One direction."
Strong: "The tabs are the steps. Work through them in order and the renewal is done."

## Consistency

- One term per thing for the whole video. If it is a "quote" in chapter 2, it is not a "candidate" in chapter 4.
- Cards in the same chapter share a grammatical shape when they can.
- Numbers as digits. Dates in the app's own format.

## Review the copy aloud

Read every card out loud at speaking pace. If it takes longer than the hold, cut words. If you stumble, restructure. If two cards in a row say the same thing, delete one.
